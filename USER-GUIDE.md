# BQMaster · Bible Quiz — User Guide

A complete guide to using the BQMaster Bible Quiz app on any device.

---

## 📖 What Is BQMaster?

BQMaster is a simple Bible quiz app that helps you test and grow your knowledge of Scripture. You choose a range of Bible verses, and the app shows you quiz questions based on that range. Each question comes with a Bible verse reference and an answer you can reveal with one tap.

The app also **reads the questions aloud**, so you can listen instead of read — great for hands-free studying, group quizzes, or accessibility.

---

## 🖥️ Getting to the App

Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox) and go to the BQMaster URL: https://<your-username>.github.io/<your-repo-name>/

No installation or sign-up is needed. The app runs entirely in your browser.

---

## 🚦 The Two Screens

BQMaster has two main screens:

1. **Setup Screen** — where you choose a verse range
2. **Quiz Screen** — where questions are displayed

The setup screen is what you see when you first open the app. The quiz screen appears after you press **Start Quizzing!**.

---

## 1️⃣ Setup Screen — Choosing Your Quiz Range

When you open the app, you'll see four dropdown menus:

| Dropdown | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Start chapter** | The chapter number where your quiz begins |
| **Start verse** | The verse number where your quiz begins |
| **End chapter** | The chapter number where your quiz ends |
| **End verse** | The verse number where your quiz ends |

### How to choose a range

1. Tap or click the **Start chapter** dropdown and pick a number.
2. The **Start verse** dropdown will automatically update to show verses from that chapter. Pick your starting verse.
3. Tap or click the **End chapter** dropdown and pick a number.
4. The **End verse** dropdown will update accordingly. Pick your ending verse.

### Example

If you want to quiz on **John 3:1 through John 3:16**:
- Start chapter: `3`
- Start verse: `1`
- End chapter: `3`
- End verse: `16`

If you want to quiz on **Genesis 1:1 through Genesis 2:10**:
- Start chapter: `1`
- Start verse: `1`
- End chapter: `2`
- End verse: `10`

### Two buttons to start

Once you've chosen your range, you have two ways to begin:

| Button | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **▶ Start Quizzing!** | Starts the quiz with questions in their original order |
| **🔀 Random Order** | Starts the quiz with the questions shuffled randomly |

Both buttons use the same verse range — the only difference is the order the questions appear in.

---

## 2️⃣ Quiz Screen — Answering Questions

Once you press **Start Quizzing!** or **Random Order**, the setup screen disappears and the quiz screen appears.

### What you see

Each question is displayed in a card with the following parts, from top to bottom:

- **Question Type** badge (green) — e.g., "Multiple Choice", "True/False"
- **Bible Verse** badge (white with 📖 icon) — the verse the question is based on
- **Question text** — the question itself, in large bold text
- **Hidden Answer box** — hidden until you reveal it
- **Three action buttons**: Read, Stop, Show Answer
- **Two navigation buttons**: Previous, Next
- **Progress bar** — shows how far through the quiz you are
- **Start Over button** — returns you to the setup screen

### Reading the question aloud

Tap the **🔊 Read** button. The app will speak:

1. The **question type** (e.g., "Multiple Choice")
2. A short pause (3 seconds)
3. The word "Question"
4. Another short pause (2 seconds)
5. The **question text**

### Stopping the reading

If you want to stop the reading at any time, tap the **⏹ Stop** button. Speech will end immediately.

> **Tip for iPhone/iPad users:** The first time you tap **Read**, iOS may ask permission for audio playback. Tap "Allow" to continue.

### Revealing the answer

Tap the **🔍 Show Answer** button. The answer will appear in a box below the question. Tap the button again (now labeled **🙈 Hide Answer**) to hide it.

The page does not reload — everything happens instantly.

### Moving between questions

Use the navigation buttons at the bottom of the card:

| Button | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **◀ Previous** | Go back to the previous question |
| **Next ▶** | Go to the next question |

The **Previous** button is disabled on the first question, and the **Next** button is disabled on the last question.

> **Tip:** When you navigate to a new question, any ongoing speech stops automatically, and the answer is hidden again.

### Tracking your progress

The **progress bar** at the bottom of the screen shows:

- A green bar that fills up as you advance
- Text like **"3 / 12"** — meaning you're on question 3 out of 12 total

### Starting over

If you want to choose a different verse range and start again, tap the **↺ Start Over** button at the very bottom. You'll be returned to the setup screen with your previous selections still in place — just adjust them and press **Start Quizzing!** again.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. Just open the app and start quizzing.

**Can I use it offline?**
Not currently — the questions are loaded from a Google Sheet when you open the page. Once loaded, you can quiz through all questions even if your connection drops, but refreshing the page requires internet again.

**Does it save my progress?**
No. If you close the page and reopen it, you'll start fresh.

**Can I share a specific question with someone?**
Not yet — this feature could be added in the future.

**Why doesn't the speech work on my phone?**
Make sure your device isn't in silent mode, and check that your browser has permission to play audio. On iOS, the first tap on **Read** will trigger a permission prompt.

**What if the app says "Could not load quiz data"?**
This means the app couldn't reach the Google Sheet. Check your internet connection and refresh the page. If the problem persists, the sheet may need to be re-published.

**Can I change the speech speed?**
Not from the app interface. The speech rate is set to a natural pace. If you need slower or faster reading, use your device's accessibility settings or system voice options.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Buttons look too big on my phone | Refresh the page. If it persists, clear your browser cache. |
| No sound when tapping **Read** | Check silent mode and volume. On iOS, tap **Read** once and allow audio when prompted. |
| Speech stops mid-sentence | This can happen if you tap **Read** again or navigate. Tap **Read** to restart. |
| Dropdowns are empty | Refresh the page — the data may still be loading. |
| Questions in a range don't appear | Your chosen range may not have any questions in the source data. Try a wider range. |
| The answer doesn't appear | Make sure you tapped **Show Answer**, not **Read**. |

## 💡 Tips for Group Study

BQMaster is great for small-group Bible study or family devotions:

- **Project the screen** on a TV or large monitor so everyone can see.
- Let **one person tap Read** so the group hears the question together.
- Give each person a turn to answer before revealing the answer.
- Use **Random Order** to keep things lively.
- Use **Previous** to revisit a question if someone wants a second try.

## 🔒 Privacy

BQMaster does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It runs entirely in your browser and only reads the quiz data from the published Google Sheet.
